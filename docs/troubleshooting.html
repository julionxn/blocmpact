    Troubleshooting and limits

    Common symptoms with their cause, and the known limits of this version.

    20. Troubleshooting

    Symptom Cause Fix
    "Full-screen effects need OpenGL" in chat Vulkan backend Graphics API → Prefer OpenGL; without it the effect keeps its other layers
    Everything burns to white on impact Energy far above 1, strong bloom, long flash Energy around 1, bloom ≤ 0.9, flash with exp(-7·s)
    The effect shows through walls It was not compared with depth Discard when t > sceneT
    Strokes flicker while moving Thinner than a pixel max(width, t·fxPixel·0.7) or the light.glsl functions
    Banding in volumes or rays Samples at fixed positions Random per-pixel start (marchVolume and lightShafts already do it)
    Jitter far from the origin Absolute coordinates in float u.world(...), frame.rel(...)
    Particles differ between players Math.random() ctx.random(), random(tick), hash(i)
    "unknown variable" in a JSON Misspelled name, or a parameter without a default Check defaults and vars
    "shader … does not compile" GLSL error The message has file:line; validate with tools/validate_fx.py
    Invisible rings or planes in a custom mesh Back-face culling Mesh.quad already faces the camera; for closed shapes, doubleSided(true)
    The effect does not show when launched by command The type does not exist on that client The client needs the mod, or the resource pack with the JSON
    A countdown is off in slow motion Timeline ticks shown as real seconds clock().realSeconds(ticks)

    21. Known limits

    • Level 3 only exists with OpenGL. If Minecraft dropped OpenGL, the passes would have to move to the official pipeline; the GLSL could be reused almost unchanged.
    • There are no real dynamic lights: lighting is faked (on screen, lightReach) or global (EnvironmentFx).
    • No music cues and no time-stretched audio without pitch change (WSOLA): with speed ≠ 1 sounds play at their normal speed.
    • It does not carve terrain: block changes are made by your ServerEffect with Minecraft's API.
    • Cloud holes follow the 12×12-block grid of Minecraft's clouds.
